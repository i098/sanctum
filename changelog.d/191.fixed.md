Context search answers questions: a query with question or helper words such as `who`, `the` or `does` ignores them, its time words and clock times, and needs only its names and its other words or their stems. The question's verb and the kind of answer it asks for are optional, so `Who owns the Falcon project?` finds `The Falcon project owner is Peter.`, `Which vendor was chosen at the start?` finds `We chose Northwind Freight for shipping.`, and `Who signed the lease?` does not return a signed contract. Questions list newest first and page with `next_cursor`, like keyword queries. A query that contains `and`, `its` or `not` is now a question too. Keyword queries without those words, and access rules, are unchanged.
