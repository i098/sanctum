Context search answers questions. A query with question or helper words such as `who`, `the` or `does` is a question: it ignores those words, time words and clock times, and needs all its names and its subject words or their stems. The question's verb, the kind of answer it asks for (`which vendor`) and the head of a `what is` or `who is` subject (`the launch date`, `the owner of onboarding`) are optional; a word after an auxiliary such as `does` or `was` is the subject until its clause names one (`When does billing start?`, `Was budget approved?`). A question that has only those optional words returns the items that match the most of them. If it names something, such as `What is the Falcon mascot?`, those words narrow the named items to the ones that match the most of them. Questions list the newest match first among equal matches and page with `next_cursor`, so `Who owns onboarding now?` lists `Marcus takes over onboarding.` before `Priya owns onboarding.`, and `Who signed the lease?` returns nothing. A keyword query that contains `and`, `its`, `not` or a possessive `'s` is now read as a question and can match more items; other keyword queries and access rules do not change.
