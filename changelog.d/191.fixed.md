Context search answers questions: a query with question or helper words such as `who`, `the` or `does` ignores them, its time words and clock times, and needs all its names and at least half of its other words or their stems. The question's verb and the kind of answer it asks for are optional; a question made only of those needs one of them. So `Who owns the Falcon project?` finds `The Falcon project owner is Peter.`, `Which shipping company did we pick?` finds `We chose Northwind Freight for shipping.`, and `Who signed the lease?` does not return a signed contract. Questions list newest first and page with `next_cursor`, like keyword queries. A query that contains `and`, `its` or `not` is now a question too. Keyword queries without those words, and access rules, are unchanged.
