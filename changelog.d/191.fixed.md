Context search answers questions: a query with question or helper words such as `who`, `the` or `does` ignores them and returns one page of items ranked by how well they match its other words and their stems, so `Who owns the Falcon project?` puts `The Falcon project owner is Peter.` first. A ranked question has no `next_cursor`, and a fact that uses none of the question's words is still not found. Keyword queries without those words and access rules are unchanged.
