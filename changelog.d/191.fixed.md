Context search answers questions. A query that contains a question or helper word, such as `who`, `the`, `does`, `we`, `us`, `can`, `and`, `not` or a possessive `'s`, is a question: it ignores those words, time words and clock times, and needs all its names and its subject words or their stems. Any keyword query that contains one of these words is now read as a question and can match more items; other keyword queries and access rules do not change. The question's verb, the kind of answer it asks for (`which vendor`), the head of a `what is` or `who is` subject (`the launch date`, `the owner of onboarding`) and the object of a helper verb in a yes/no question (`Does onboarding have an owner?`) are optional. A word after an auxiliary such as `does` or `was` is the subject until its clause names one (`When does billing start?`, `Was budget approved?`). A question that has only optional words lists the items that contain one of them, or the items it names (`What is the Falcon mascot?`), and ranks items that contain more of those words first. Within one rank the newest item comes first, and `next_cursor` keeps that order across pages. So `Who owns onboarding now?` lists `Marcus takes over onboarding.` before `Priya owns onboarding.`, and `Who signed the lease?` returns nothing. Cursors from earlier versions are no longer accepted.
